# Spooky Diner Rush — CrazyGames Release Notes

**Release source:** `main`  
**Release commit:** `7ffb72f` (merge of progression, Taco variety and new ghost customers)

## What's new in this update

### Faster progression
- Ice cream now unlocks at level 8 (was 12).
- The Taco Truck now unlocks at level 10 for 1,500 coins (was level 15 for 2,000).
- Impatient ghosts show up a little earlier (level 9).

### Smarter Taco Truck orders
- Customers avoid ordering the same dish as the customer before them, and no order holds the same dish twice (from menu step 3).
- Orders are balanced across tacos, burritos, nachos and drinks, so no single kind takes over.
- Bigger three-item orders come in gradually as the menu grows.
- New dishes are still introduced one at a time, and learning speed is unchanged.
- Existing saves keep all progress; nothing is reset.

### Three new ghost customers
- Blue Yeti (from level 3), Pink Fuzz (from level 5) and Mummy Ghost (from level 7), each with calm, waiting, angry and happy animations.
- The Taco Truck keeps its original ghost.
- New ghost art loads in the background one level ahead, so the first download stays small. Devices with little memory keep the original ghost.

### Unchanged
- Reopen rewards (55 / 100 reputation), the 180-second ad cooldown and all ad placements.

## Gameplay and progression

### Burn Guard (unlocks at level 7)
- Adds three upgrade tiers that extend the burn window for fryer vats, grill burners, and the oven.
- Upgrade costs: 150 / 300 / 500 coins.
- Burn-window multipliers: 1.15× / 1.30× / 1.45×.
- Food can still burn; existing cooking and danger feedback remain active.
- Does not affect drinks or ice cream.

### Calm Kitchen (unlocks at level 11)
- Adds two upgrade tiers that reduce the reputation penalty from burnt dishes.
- Upgrade costs: 250 / 450 coins.
- Burnt-dish reputation penalty is reduced progressively (divisors 1.14× and 1.33×).
- Does not change customer patience, angry departures, trash penalties, or Reopen rewards.

## CrazyGames launch safeguards
- Ads are disabled by default unless explicitly enabled for an approved test/configuration.
- Adds timeout and recovery safeguards around ad-start flows.
- Prevents duplicate queued Reopen actions and handles late ad callbacks more safely.
- Cleans up story-popup overlays and tweens when they close.

## Compatibility and scope
- Existing recipes, customer behavior, controls, cooking timers, save format, and core progression are retained.
- The CrazyGames build target omits the intro video and source-only ghost PNGs to reduce package size.
